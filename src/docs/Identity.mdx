## Two inks, one accent

Light mode sets ink <TokenChip token="color.light.ink" /> on a white page
<TokenChip token="color.light.bg" /> with neutral panels
<TokenChip token="color.light.panel" />. Dark mode is neutral near-black
<TokenChip token="color.dark.bg" /> and climbs lighter.

The one accent is ochre <TokenChip token="color.brand.ochre" /> (brand
refresh, 2026-09-22), and it is a fill: always with ink text (9.01:1), never
text or a hairline on light. On light, ochre lines and the focus ring are
ochre-deep <TokenChip token="color.brand.ochre-deep" />. Links and buttons
are ink; the ochre ring means focus, and only that. Iris and periwinkle are
retired.

The brand pattern (an animal-print collage on ochre) lives in the wordmark
and, in quiet grey, behind page openings. See Brand.

## Figtree, set for reading, and a wordmark

Type is Figtree (type lock for readability, Elleta, 2026-10-04), in five sizes:

- **Headings** are Figtree 500, sentence case, through the Heading component:
  H1 44 to 68px, H2 34 to 46px, with only slight negative tracking.
- **Lead** is 21 to 24px, **body** 20 to 22px at line height 1.7, **small** 18px.
  Body and captions are `text-body`, a near-black ink, never grey.
- **Labels, buttons and meta** are Figtree at 16px or more, sentence case, no
  caps and no tracking. Button labels are Figtree SemiBold 16px.
- **Geist Mono** is for real code and token names only, at 16px.
- **Unique** is the wordmark and nothing else.

## Surfaces: flat cards, lift means clickable

The light page is pure white, and that is the only place white is allowed.
Cards and panels are one step down <TokenChip token="color.semantic.surface-card" />
with a 1px hairline <TokenChip token="color.semantic.border-faint" /> and no
shadow at rest. Colour lives in fills, never in strokes or body text, except
focus and status (danger, success). Only an interactive card lifts, 2px with a soft shadow, on
hover and focus; a static card never moves. Dark elevation climbs lighter
on neutral greys: the page, then surface <TokenChip token="color.dark.surface" />,
inset <TokenChip token="color.dark.inset" />, and raised <TokenChip token="color.dark.raised" />. One light source,
upper-left: highlights top-left, shadows down-right. The orb and keycap
elevation tokens are a lock; that depth IS the system.

## The stance

Editorial. Confident. Closer to a magazine or a well-set book than a SaaS
dashboard. When in doubt, more type, less chrome.
