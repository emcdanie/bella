## Two inks, one accent

Light mode sits ink <TokenChip token="color.brand.ink" /> on warm off-white
ground <TokenChip token="color.brand.ground" />. Dark mode is a warm
near-black ground <TokenChip token="color.night.ground" />, not navy. Navy
<TokenChip token="color.brand.navy" /> stays for the site footer, the label
on dark accent fills, and light-mode inverse surfaces.

The single accent theme-flips: iris <TokenChip token="color.brand.iris" /> in
light, periwinkle <TokenChip token="color.brand.periwinkle" /> in dark. They
are one accent seen in two modes, never two accents, never paired with a
second accent colour. Each fails its opposite ground so hard that it is
banned there even as decoration. At body scale the accent means INTERACTIVE,
and only that.

## Geist, three cuts, and a wordmark

Type is Geist (style unify, 2026-09-22), in three cuts plus Mono:

- **Headings** are Geist Light 300 with tight tracking (−0.035em display,
  −0.03em for the section h2), sentence case, through the Heading component.
  Section headings never drop below 32px.
- **Titles** are Geist 500 at 20px. **Body** is Geist 400 at 17px with
  −0.01em tracking.
- **Eyebrows and meta labels** are Geist Mono 400 at 13px, stylistic set 9,
  no tracking, no caps. Tag and StatusPill use it; Button, Select, NavLink and
  form labels stay Geist.
- **Unique** is the wordmark and nothing else.

## Surfaces: flat cards, lift means clickable

The light page is pure white, and that is the only place white is allowed.
Cards and panels are one step down <TokenChip token="color.semantic.surface-card" />
with a 1px hairline <TokenChip token="color.semantic.border-faint" /> and no
shadow at rest. Colour lives in fills, never in strokes or body text, except
the focus ring. Only an interactive card lifts, 2px with a soft shadow, on
hover and focus; a static card never moves. Dark elevation climbs lighter
on neutral greys: the page, then surface <TokenChip token="color.dark.surface" />,
inset <TokenChip token="color.dark.inset" />, and raised <TokenChip token="color.dark.raised" />. One light source,
upper-left: highlights top-left, shadows down-right. The orb and keycap
elevation tokens are a lock; that depth IS the system.

## The stance

Editorial. Confident. Closer to a magazine or a well-set book than a SaaS
dashboard. When in doubt, more type, less chrome.
