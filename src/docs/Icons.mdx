## The registry

Everything BELLA ships, at the three ramp steps (sm 16, md 20, lg 24),
in the context ink. Adding an icon means adding its Iconoir paths to
`src/components/Icon/registry.ts`; nothing renders inline, and
audit:quality fails any `<svg>` outside the Icon component.

## The rules

- **currentColor, always.** An icon inherits the ink of its text context;
  dark mode recolours it for free. A hardcoded icon colour is a bug.
- **Decorative by default.** Icons render aria-hidden. A meaningful icon
  requires the `label` prop (role img), and it never stands without text
  unless its accessible name is proven in a Behavior story.
- **The ramp is the sizing model.** sm 16 / md 20 / lg 24 plus the stroke
  token; no ad-hoc icon sizes.
