## Two inks, one accent

Light mode sets ink <TokenChip token="color.light.ink" /> on a #F7F7F5 page
ground <TokenChip token="color.light.bg" /> with white cards (shared look,
8 Nov 2026). Dark mode is neutral near-black
<TokenChip token="color.dark.bg" /> and climbs lighter.

The brand accent is ochre <TokenChip token="color.brand.ochre" /> (brand
refresh, 2026-09-22), and it is a fill: always with ink text (9.01:1), never
text or a hairline on light. It marks the "Needs you" state. On light, ochre
lines are ochre-deep <TokenChip token="color.brand.ochre-deep" />. Links and
buttons are ink. The focus ring is ink in light and near-white in dark, and
means focus, only that (shared look, 8 Nov 2026). Selection is the pink fill
#E58BBB with a near-black label, 8.13:1. Iris and periwinkle are retired.

The brand pattern (an animal-print collage on ochre) lives in the wordmark
and, in quiet grey, behind page openings. See Brand.

## Newsreader and DM Sans, set for reading, and a wordmark

Headings are Newsreader and everything else is DM Sans (shared look, 8 Nov
2026). The sizes stay as the type lock for readability set them (Elleta,
2026-10-04), in five sizes:

- **Headings** are Newsreader 500, sentence case, through the Heading component:
  H1 44 to 60px, H2 34 to 40px, with only slight negative tracking.
- **Lead** is 21 to 22px, **body** 20px at line height 1.7, **small** 18px.
  Body and captions are `text-body`, a near-black ink, never grey.
- **Labels, buttons and meta** are DM Sans at 16px or more, sentence case, no
  caps and no tracking. Button labels are DM Sans 16px.
- **Geist Mono** is for real code and token names only, at 16px.
- **Unique** is the wordmark and nothing else.

## Surfaces: flat cards, lift means clickable

The light page ground is #F7F7F5 and cards are white (shared look, 8 Nov
2026). Cards <TokenChip token="color.semantic.surface-card" />
carry a 1px hairline <TokenChip token="color.semantic.border-faint" /> and no
shadow at rest. Colour lives in fills, never in strokes or body text, except
focus and status (danger, success). Only an interactive card lifts, 2px with a soft shadow, on
hover and focus; a static card never moves. Dark elevation climbs lighter
on neutral greys: the page, then surface <TokenChip token="color.dark.surface" />,
inset <TokenChip token="color.dark.inset" />, and raised <TokenChip token="color.dark.raised" />. One light source,
upper-left: highlights top-left, shadows down-right. The orb
elevation tokens are a lock. The primary Button is flat and never lifts
(shared look, 8 Nov 2026), so the key shadow tokens keep their names but no
longer dress it.

## The stance

Editorial. Confident. Closer to a magazine or a well-set book than a SaaS
dashboard. When in doubt, more type, less chrome.
