## Two inks, one accent

Light mode sits ink <TokenChip token="color.brand.ink" /> on warm off-white
ground <TokenChip token="color.brand.ground" />. Dark mode is navy
<TokenChip token="color.brand.navy" />, not black.

The single accent theme-flips: iris <TokenChip token="color.brand.iris" /> in
light, periwinkle <TokenChip token="color.brand.periwinkle" /> in dark. They
are one accent seen in two modes, never two accents, never paired with a
second accent colour. Each fails its opposite ground so hard that it is
banned there even as decoration. At body scale the accent means INTERACTIVE,
and only that.

## Two faces

Type is Unique and Geist, exactly two faces:

- **Unique** (700 is the only cut) is display: page-tier headings, the home
  hero headline, the keycap brand lockup. Never below 24px, never negative
  tracking.
- **Geist** is everything else: body, UI, card titles, eyebrows, nav,
  buttons, chips. Eyebrows are Geist caps with wide tracking; the tracking is
  the look.
- The mono face is retired. Legacy consumers get Geist through the old token.

## Surfaces: lift, not darkness

Pure white is banned as a solid fill. The canvas is ground; cards and raised
surfaces are paper <TokenChip token="color.neutral.paper" />, separated from
the page by lift and shadow, not by darker fills. Dark elevation climbs
lighter: navy page, then card <TokenChip token="color.navy.card" />, then
raised <TokenChip token="color.navy.raised" />. One light source, upper-left:
highlights top-left, shadows down-right. The elevation tokens are a lock; the
depth IS the system.

## The stance

Editorial. Confident. Closer to a magazine or a well-set book than a SaaS
dashboard. When in doubt, more type, less chrome.
