# Accessibility

The bar is **AAA-minded AA**, recorded as rule 9 in `docs/RULES.md`
(Elleta, 2026-07-21). It is a recorded decision, not a vibe: the ratios are
computed, written into the tokens, and enforced downstream.

## The bar

- **AAA for ink and body text.** Both ink ladders are verified AAA on every
  surface they sit on. Body text never drops below AAA.
- **AA where the accent speaks.** Accent text, buttons, and links are
  AA-verified per token. AAA accent text is not attainable with iris on light
  surfaces and is not the bar.
- **The accent always theme-flips.** Iris in light, periwinkle in dark. Each
  fails its opposite ground so hard that it is banned there even as
  decoration; the failure includes the 3:1 non-text minimum.
- **Worst-ground-wins.** A text token passes AA normal text on the worst
  surface its usage metadata allows, or that surface is forbidden in the
  metadata.

Beyond contrast: touch targets hold WCAG 2.5.5 Enhanced (44px minimum via
`spacing.touch-target`), every story in this Storybook runs axe with
serious and critical violations failing the build, and each component ships
with a behavioral suite (keyboard, focus, states) before it lands.
