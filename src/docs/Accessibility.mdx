## The bar

- **AAA for ink and body text.** Both ink ladders are verified AAA on every
  surface they sit on. Body text never drops below AAA.
- **Ochre is a fill.** It always carries ink text (9.01:1) and is never
  text, a hairline or a ring on light (2.08:1 on white). Links and button
  labels are ink, so they hold AAA.
- **The ring means focus.** 3px with a 3px offset: ochre-deep in light
  (3.20:1 on panel), ochre in dark (8.70:1). Never at rest. Every declared
  pair is recomputed in both themes on each gate run.
- **Worst-ground-wins.** A text token passes AA normal text on the worst
  surface its usage metadata allows, or that surface is forbidden in the
  metadata.

Beyond contrast: touch targets hold WCAG 2.5.5 Enhanced (44px minimum via
`spacing.touch-target`), every story in this Storybook runs axe with
serious and critical violations failing the build, and each component ships
with a behavioral suite (keyboard, focus, states) before it lands.
