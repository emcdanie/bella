## ELLETA and BELLA

Full-bleed, the wordmark fills its container's width and sits on the
baseline: nothing hangs below the letters, so it closes a footer cleanly.

<Canvas of={WordmarkStories.Elleta} />
<Canvas of={WordmarkStories.Bella} />

## Pattern and ink

The pattern is the default. Ink is the fallback where the pattern would
fight what is around it, or where colour is not available. Ink strokes in
<TokenChip token="color.semantic.text-primary" />.

<Canvas of={WordmarkStories.Ink} />

## In the nav

The nav wordmark is 40px tall. Below 32px the pattern turns to noise, so
that is the floor.

<Canvas of={WordmarkStories.NavSize} />

## Reroll

The pattern is drawn from a seed, and the same seed always draws the same
pattern, so a page renders the same on every visit. Pass `onReroll` to add a
Reroll control that picks a new seed.

<Canvas of={WordmarkStories.Reroll} />
<Controls of={WordmarkStories.Elleta} />

## The glyph sheet

Each glyph on a 100-unit cap height, with its centre line: the path the
stroke follows. The locked settings are weight 12, width 58, height 130,
tracking 16 and soft corners. The E, A and B bars all sit on one low line.

<Canvas of={WordmarkStories.GlyphSheet} />

## Do and don't

<DoDont
  doItems={[

  dontItems={[
    "Don't put it below 32px tall",
